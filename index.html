Chae-Yeon Heo

3D Vision Engineer

Research Intern, Spatial AI Team, NAVER LABS

Computer vision and generative AI researcher focused on making visual AI systems more controllable and reliable in the real world. My work connects high-level semantic understanding with practical, controllable image manipulation, spanning image restoration, inpainting, and generative editing, and I'm currently expanding into 3D vision, including 3D Gaussian Splatting and neural rendering.

I'm a Research Intern at NAVER LABS, working on 3D vision and generative AI, and hold an M.S. in AI Convergence from Chonnam National University, advised by Prof. Yeong-Jun Cho.

Open to research collaborations and conversations about vision and generative modeling. Feel free to reach out.
